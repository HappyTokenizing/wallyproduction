The RWA Foundation

The coordination layer for the tokenized economy.

The RWA Foundation is the dedicated nonprofit for the onchain economy and the steward of WALLY NFT. We exist for one reason: to make Real-World Assets onchain trustworthy, understood, and open to everyone.

Membership

Our members don't buy a seat.
They stand for something.

The companies above are builders of the RWA economy, and they choose to support an independent foundation. Not for placement, not for favors: because an industry with neutral ground, honest data and real education grows faster for everyone, themselves included.

That's the whole deal. When members support the mission, the mission strengthens the industry. A stronger industry is the win they share in. Everyone wins, by design.

They fund open education

Member support keeps every primer, course and data explainer free for the whole industry, not a paying few.
They back honest data

Our members underwrite independent research and reporting, and they accept the numbers even when the numbers aren't flattering.
They show up for the work

Supporting the development of our education, advocacy and onboarding initiatives to help the RWA Foundation achieve its mission.
How we operate

Three rules.
No exceptions.

PRINCIPLE · 01
Neutrality

We don't pick winners. No member, however generous, shapes our findings, our rankings, or our voice. Support buys the industry a stronger foundation; it never buys an outcome.

PRINCIPLE · 02
Truth seeking

We verify before we publish, source everything, and correct ourselves in public when we get it wrong. Correct information is the foundation the whole industry stands on.

PRINCIPLE · 03
Mission only

Every dollar and every decision serves one purpose: bringing financial services to the people of the planet: saving, yield, credit and ownership, open to everyone. Trustworthy RWAs onchain are how it happens. We hold no other agenda.

Believe the industry deserves neutral ground?

Then we're already on the same side. Member companies join because the mission matters. The conversation starts there.

WALLY

The face of open finance, and a mission to bring the whole planet onchain through fair, decentralized access to real-world assets.

Scroll

WALLY isn’t just a regular elephant.

He’s on a mission to bring all people onchain through open and fair RWA access, for the whole planet.

Real-world assets are the bridge to financial services, ones that belong on open rails. WALLY’s job is to make that world economy reachable by anyone with a wallet: no gatekeepers, no borders, no minimum entry required.

Three reasons he’s an elephant.

01The size As big as the world

The whole economy is coming onchain.

An elephant is the largest animal that walks the earth, and the RWA opportunity is the largest in finance. Trillions of dollars of real world value, the entire world economy, is moving onto open networks. WALLY is sized to match the scale of what’s at stake.

The prize is the whole balance sheet of the planet.

02The mind Smart by nature

Wise enough to rebuild finance.

Elephants are among the most intelligent creatures alive: they remember, they plan, they solve. Rebuilding markets in the open takes the same intelligence: sound engineering, audited smart contracts, and clear eyed decisions made together by the Herd. WALLY is built to be as smart as the systems he’s replacing.

Open finance only works when it’s engineered with care.

03The herd Never alone

Nobody crosses alone.

Elephants move as a herd by traveling together between watering holes, keeping the young in the middle, protecting the whole pack. That’s the shape of this movement: shared mission, shared knowledge, and members who look out for one another. Onchain, the watering holes are open markets... and everyone is invited to drink!

We move together, or we will be left out.

DeFi’s new face is RWA powered.

WALLY stands for open, decentralized and fair access to markets. Wall Street was built to control the financial system, and that grip only loosens when the power is handed back to people, through tokenization and open, permissionless rails.

WALLY

He’s here to break down the walls of Wall Street.

Token fundamentals

Crypto meets sustainable RWA growth.

Every WALLY is a working license: it posts capacity in the capacity market, it votes, and it earns weight with time. Behind it, an operating reserve of real assets and an engine the community must earn. Utility first. Decentralization second. Conversion third.

1B supply, hard cap 500M in the token treasury 100+ year horizon
